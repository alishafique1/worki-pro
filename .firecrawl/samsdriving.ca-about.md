###### Get to Know Us

### High-Quality Driving Training at Affordable Prices

Customer-first service with a very high first-time pass rate.

We specialize in all aspects of driving training, from MTO-approved beginner driver education to road test preparation and refresher lessons. Our fully-qualified instructors guide you step-by-step so you feel prepared and confident on test day. Lessons are delivered to a high standard in dual brake control vehicles.

### Why Choose Sam’s Driving School?

We’ve grown from our first Scarborough office to multiple branches across the GTA. We keep training practical, supportive, and focused on real-world driving skills.
